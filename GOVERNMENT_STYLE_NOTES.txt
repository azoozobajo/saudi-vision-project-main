Saudi Government-Style UI Version
=================================

Direction
---------
This version was redesigned to feel closer to a formal Saudi government digital presentation:
- Cleaner white backgrounds and official green leadership.
- Less decorative glassmorphism and fewer heavy gradients.
- More structured navigation, rectangular tabs, formal cards, clear borders, and controlled shadows.
- Gold used for formal separators and emphasis.
- Purple used only as a limited modern transformation accent, not as a dominant identity color.

Requested palette
-----------------
Green:  #006C35
White:  #FFFFFF
Gold:   #C5A358
Purple: #913191
