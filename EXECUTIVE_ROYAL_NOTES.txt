Executive / Royal UI Redesign
=============================

Design direction
----------------
- Built a more premium, leadership-facing version of the proposal website.
- Preserved the existing section order, content structure, Arabic/English switching, and functional flow.
- Shifted the look from calm government-formal to executive/royal:
  - deep emerald / Saudi green atmosphere
  - stronger gold highlights
  - richer shadows and larger premium cards
  - controlled purple accent for transformation / prestige
  - elevated hero section and navigation

Palette used
------------
- Saudi green: #006C35
- White: #FFFFFF
- Gold: #C5A358
- Purple: #913191
